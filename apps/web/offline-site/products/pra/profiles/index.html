PRA/Profiles

PRA profiles

Configurations are claims. Profiles make them testable.

A PRA profile binds routing, selection, materialization, model topology, and runtime settings to a stated objective and evidence gate.

REFERENCE_CORRECTNESS

Available
Objective
Reproduce mechanism and parity controls.
Quality gate
Exact tested-path invariants and geometry-matched reference behavior.
Tradeoff
Not optimized for serving cost.

QUALITY_MAX

Experimental
Objective
Maximize validated task quality.
Quality gate
No material regression against the strongest qualified baseline.
Tradeoff
May use more selected context and memory.

QUALITY_MAX_CANDIDATE

Candidate
Objective
Evaluate an unpromoted quality-oriented setting.
Quality gate
Must pass held-out and serving qualification before promotion.
Tradeoff
Research-only until evidence closes.

BALANCED

Candidate
Objective
Balance quality, active K/V, and latency.
Quality gate
Profile-specific quality floor plus infrastructure benefit.
Tradeoff
Combination-specific; not universal.

ECONOMY

Candidate
Objective
Minimize active context and serving cost.
Quality gate
Successful-task quality must remain within the declared bound.
Tradeoff
Higher miss risk and stricter fallback needs.

Promotion

Candidate → measured → recommended

A profile becomes a recommendation only for the combinations it has actually passed.
