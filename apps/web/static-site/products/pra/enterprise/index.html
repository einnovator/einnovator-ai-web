Commercial work builds on the open assessment workflow.

The public CLI creates the workload configuration, environment receipt, quality evidence, metrics, recommendation, and report. A Comprehensive Evaluation adds jointly defined gates, production-like traffic, specialist analysis, customer-specific calibration, capacity economics, an acceptance suite, and a scoped recommendation.

Method

Baseline → agreed gates → measurement → recommendation.

01

Baseline Full Context

Pin the model, engine, hardware, workload, traffic shape, and current prompt strategy.

02

Freeze quality and evidence

Agree on task success, safety constraints, datasets, statistical treatment, and fallback thresholds.

03

Measure Selected Context

Route and select ordinary context, then measure tokens, quality, TTFT, throughput, memory, and successful work.

04

Qualify Native Memory

Only when reuse is relevant, freeze selection and measure incremental encoding, transfer, memory, and serving effects.

05

Qualify Native Serving

Only when the engine owns the seam, measure prefetch, residency, transfer, tails, and useful throughput.

06

Recommend mode and profile

Choose the shallowest path that improves the measured quality-cost frontier with a clear fallback.

07

Deliver qualification artifacts

Provide results, limitations, an acceptance suite, capacity model, and production implementation plan.

FAQ and procurement

Indicative anchors, explicit scope.

What are the common engagement ranges?

Comprehensive Evaluations are commonly €5K–€20K. Production Implementations are commonly €20K–€50K+. Control Plane enablement is commonly +€10K–€30K when applicable. Support ranges from €10K to €100K+/year depending on scope and service requirements.

Are these contractual prices?

No. They are planning anchors. A proposal defines the workload, deliverables, environments, acceptance criteria, schedule, dependencies, and commercial terms.

Does an evaluation guarantee savings?

No. It guarantees the scoped process and deliverables: baseline, agreed gates, measurement, recommendation, limitations, and reproduction. A negative recommendation is valid.

Do we have to buy services to use PRA?

No. PRA remains open source. Teams can evaluate and implement it independently using the public runtime, tooling, integrations, bundles, and evidence.

How are Design Partners different?

Design partnerships are separate, selective collaborations based on technical fit and mutual research or product value. They are not the standard route to discounted or free consulting.
